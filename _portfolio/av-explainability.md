Built a multimodal pipeline generating real-time, natural-language explanations of autonomous-vehicle driving decisions for passengers, to study trust and comfort (N=22 user study). TUM × Continental AG, June 2025.

Benchmarked 6 vision-language models (BLIP, CLIP, LLaVA 1.5, LLaMA 4, Kimi-VL) for driving-scene captioning, selected and deployed Kimi-VL based on speed/accuracy trade-offs, and layered an LLM refinement stage (GPT-4o, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Claude 3.7 Sonnet) converting verbose captions into concise in-cabin explanations with subtitle and TTS output modes.
