Groundwater level forecasting for three Prince Edward Island watersheds, using Prophet, BiLSTM and GRU driven by meteorological and hydrological variables. Information-theoretic feature selection, chronological train/test splits, and confidence intervals — Prophet held NSE above 0.90 in every watershed.

Published as first author and used in provincial climate adaptation policy for the Government of Prince Edward Island.
